# Project Timeline

## September — Project Definition and Data Acquisition

* Finalize project scope and research question.
* Complete advisor touchpoint and incorporate advisor feedback.
* Identify and evaluate candidate EHR/clinical datasets.
* Confirm dataset accessibility and research-use requirements.
* Finalize the prediction task and initial experimental design.
* Establish baseline methodology.

## October — Data Processing and Baseline Modeling

* Complete exploratory data analysis.
* Analyze missing-data patterns.
* Develop preprocessing pipeline.
* Implement baseline imputation approach.
* Train and evaluate baseline machine learning models.
* Begin implementation of the uncertainty-aware methodology.

## November — Experiments and Evaluation

* Complete uncertainty-aware modeling approach.
* Conduct experiments comparing baseline and uncertainty-aware methods.
* Evaluate predictive performance.
* Analyze prediction errors and uncertainty estimates.
* Conduct additional robustness or sensitivity analyses as appropriate.
* Begin documenting final results.

## December — Final Evaluation and Deliverables

* Complete final experiments and analysis.
* Generate final figures and tables.
* Interpret results and document findings.
* Complete final project report.
* Prepare final presentation and other course deliverables.
* Clean and document the GitHub repository.

## Key Dependencies

The final dataset, prediction outcome, and specific uncertainty methodology will be finalized after dataset evaluation and advisor feedback. The timeline may be adjusted as these decisions are confirmed.
